About the Artist

Harold Trinidad

Professional Saxophonist

Creating Unforgettable Live Musical Memories

Harold Trinidad is a renowned professional saxophonist based in Pampanga, Philippines. Known for his soulful tone, emotional depth, and versatile repertoire, Harold elevates weddings, corporate galas, and milestone celebrations.

Whether performing romantic romantic covers during church ceremonies, ambient background jazz for cocktail hour, or high-energy solos for wedding receptions, Harold ensures every performance leaves a lasting impression on you and your guests.

Booking Policy

Terms & Conditions

Important details regarding date reservation, performance sets, and song requests.

  • ◆
    Date Reservation:

    Your event date will be officially secured upon receipt of the 30% non-refundable downpayment (₱2,550 for Package A / ₱1,650 for Package B). This ensures your date is exclusively reserved.

  • ◆
    Coverage & Transport Fees:

    Rates apply to bookings within the Pampanga Area (includes a fixed ₱500 transport fee). Events outside the Pampanga area are subject to adjusted rates and additional travel fees. Contact us for a customized quotation.

  • ◆
    Performance Duration:

    Each set consists of one full hour of continuous live performance. Package A includes 2 Sets (2 Hours); Package B includes 1 Set (1 Hour).

  • ◆
    Song Requests & Finalization:

    Please submit your finalized list of special song requests at least one month prior to your event date.

  • ◆
    Custom Arrangements:

    Special songs outside our standard repertoire may require a modest arrangement fee of ₱500 per song.
